欢迎使用「熊猫拼字：冲冲冲！」（以下简称“本应用”或“本游戏”）。我们非常重视您的隐私保护。本隐私政策旨在向您清晰说明本应用在您游玩过程中如何处理、存储数据，以及所集成的第三方服务（Google AdMob）如何使用相关信息。

1. 开发者直接收集的信息与本地存储

本应用无需注册账号，不设用户登录系统，开发者本人不会收集、上传或存储您的姓名、电话号码、电子邮箱、通讯录、照片或精确地理位置等个人身份信息。

为了保障正常的游戏体验，本应用仅在您的设备本地（通过系统标准本地存储 shared_preferences）保存以下游戏进度与设置数据：

  • 关卡闯关进度、已解锁章节及每关星级记录
  • 当前体力值（爱心数量）及体力自然恢复时间戳
  • 音效、背景音乐、震动反馈开关及难度偏好设置

上述数据仅保存在您的手机或平板设备本地，不会上传至任何开发者服务器。

2. 第三方 SDK 与广告服务（Google AdMob）

本应用是一款免费休闲益智游戏，集成了 Google 提供的 Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob, google_mobile_ads) 用于展示横幅广告、插页广告及激励视频广告（例如：观看激励广告补充体力或获取游戏内提示）。

为实现广告展示、频次控制、反欺诈及广告效果衡量，Google AdMob SDK 可能会自动收集并处理以下设备与使用数据：

数据类型 具体说明 用途
设备与广告标识符 iOS 广告标识符（IDFA，仅在您通过 ATT 弹窗明确授权时收集）、Android 广告 ID（AAID）、应用实例 ID 第三方广告投放、频次控制、广告归因与分析
粗略位置信息 基于 IP 地址推断的大致国家/地区或城市（不收集 GPS 精确位置） 投放符合地区语言的合规广告、防范广告欺诈
产品交互与广告数据 广告展示次数、点击交互、激励广告完播状态、应用启动与基础交互事件 广告效果评估、发放激励奖励、防作弊统计
诊断与性能数据 崩溃日志、广告加载耗时、设备型号、操作系统版本、屏幕尺寸 提升 SDK 稳定性、排查崩溃与性能问题

有关 Google 如何收集、处理和保护数据的详细说明，请查阅 Google 官方政策：

  • Google 隐私权政策：https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google 如何使用来自采用其服务的网站和应用的信息：https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • AdMob 与 Google Play / Apple 隐私披露说明：https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

3. 您的隐私选择与如何关闭个性化广告

您可以随时管理或撤回对广告跟踪与个性化广告的授权，这不会影响您正常游玩本游戏的核心功能：

  • iOS / iPadOS 用户（App Tracking Transparency）：首次启动应用时，系统会弹出「是否允许跟踪」询问窗口；您若选择“要求 App 不跟踪”，AdMob 将不会访问您的 IDFA，仅展示非个性化广告。您也可以随时在设备「设置 > 隐私与安全性 > 跟踪」中关闭本应用的跟踪权限。
  • Android 用户：您可以前往设备「设置 > Google > 广告」（或「设置 > 隐私 > 广告」），选择“重置广告 ID”或“删除广告 ID / 关闭广告个性化”。
  • 游戏内隐私设置（欧盟/英国等适用 GDPR/UMP 地区）：您可以打开游戏右上角「设置」弹窗，点击「广告隐私设置」随时修改或撤回您的同意选项。

4. 数据保留与如何删除您的数据

  • 本地游戏数据：由于游戏进度与设置仅保存在您的设备本地，您只需在系统设置中清除本应用的缓存/存储数据，或直接卸载本应用，即可立即、彻底删除所有本地游戏数据。
  • 广告标识符数据：您可通过上文第 3 节所述方式在系统设置中重置或删除设备的广告标识符（IDFA / AAID）。

5. 未成年人/儿童隐私保护

本应用为大众休闲益智文字游戏（年龄分级 4+），不包含任何暴力、赌博、社交聊天或用户生成内容（UGC）。我们不会主动收集任何未成年人或儿童的个人身份信息。当用户未授予跟踪权限时，应用仅请求非个性化情境广告。如家长或监护人发现任何疑问，请通过下方联系方式与我们联系。

6. 隐私政策的变更

我们可能会适时更新本隐私政策，更新后的版本将直接发布在本页面并更新顶部的“最近更新”日期。重大变更将在应用更新说明或应用内予以提示。

Welcome to Panda Hanzi: Chinese Match ("the App" or "the Game"). We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about how data is handled when you play the Game.

1. Information Collected by the Developer & Local Storage

The App does not require account registration or user sign-in. The developer does not collect, upload, or store any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise GPS location) on any developer-owned server.

To save your game progress, the App stores the following data locally on your device only (using standard on-device key-value storage, shared_preferences):

  • Level progress, unlocked chapters, and star ratings
  • Current hearts (energy) count and heart regeneration timestamps
  • Audio, music, vibration/haptics preferences, and difficulty settings

All of this data remains strictly on your device and is never transmitted to the developer.

2. Third-Party SDK & Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep the Game free to play, we integrate the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob, google_mobile_ads) to display banner ads, interstitial ads, and rewarded ads (for example, when you choose to watch an ad to refill hearts).

Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain device and usage data for ad delivery, frequency capping, fraud prevention, and analytics:

Data Category Details Purpose
Device & Advertising Identifiers iOS Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA, collected only if you grant permission via the iOS App Tracking Transparency prompt), Android Advertising ID (AAID), App Set ID Third-party advertising, frequency capping, attribution, and analytics
Coarse Location Approximate country/region or city inferred from IP address (no precise GPS location is collected) Geo-relevant ad delivery and fraud prevention
Product Interaction & Ad Data Ad impressions, clicks, rewarded video completions, and basic app launch/session events Ad performance measurement, granting in-game rewards, and anti-fraud
Diagnostics & Performance Crash logs, ad load latency, device model, OS version, screen dimensions SDK stability, crash reporting, and performance diagnostics

For full details on how Google collects and processes data, please review Google's official privacy documentation:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • AdMob & App Privacy Disclosures: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

3. Opting Out of Personalized Ads & Tracking

You have full control over ad tracking and personalization at any time:

  • iOS / iPadOS (App Tracking Transparency): When prompted on iOS, you can select "Ask App Not to Track". You can also toggle tracking permission at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. When tracking is disabled, IDFA is not accessed and only contextual/non-personalized ads are requested.
  • Android: Open Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads) and select "Reset advertising ID" or "Delete advertising ID" / opt out of Ads Personalization.
  • In-App Privacy Settings (GDPR / UMP Regions): Where applicable, open the in-game Settings (设置) sheet and tap Ad Privacy Settings (广告隐私设置) to review or withdraw your consent at any time.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

  • Local Game Data: Because all game progress and settings are stored solely on your device, you can permanently delete all local data at any time by clearing the App's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the App.
  • Advertising Identifiers: You can reset or delete your device's advertising identifier at any time in your iOS or Android system settings as described in Section 3.

5. Children's Privacy

Panda Hanzi is a general-audience casual puzzle game (rated 4+) with no user accounts, chat, or user-generated content. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. When tracking authorization is not granted, only non-personalized ads are requested. If you are a parent or guardian and have any concerns, please contact us using the details below.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
