关于「熊猫拼字：冲冲冲！」

「熊猫拼字：冲冲冲！」（Panda Hanzi: Chinese Match，Bundle ID: com.pandahanzi.game）是一款国潮风格的汉字偏旁部首拆字连连看益智游戏。在棋盘上找到能拼成一个常用汉字的两块部件（例如「冫」+「中」=「冲」），用不超过两个拐弯的连线将它们连接即可消除过关！

常见问题 (FAQ)

Q1：游戏怎么玩？遇到不会拼的字怎么办？

在棋盘中点击两个可以组合成一个完整汉字的部件，只要它们之间的连线拐弯不超过两次即可消除。每局支持使用「提示」（直接高亮一对可拼汉字）和「洗牌 / 换一盘」道具；消除成功后还会展示该汉字的拼音与释义，边玩边认字。

Q2：体力（爱心）怎么恢复？

体力上限为 5 颗爱心。当体力不满 5 颗时，每 20 分钟会自动恢复 1 颗爱心；您也可以点击顶栏爱心图标，通过观看一段激励视频广告立即免费补充体力。

Q3：如何关闭音效、背景音乐或震动？

点击游戏主界面或关卡界面右上角的「设置」按钮，即可独立开关「音效」「音乐」与「震动」反馈。

Q4：游戏需要联网或注册账号吗？进度保存在哪里？

本游戏无需注册或登录账号，核心关卡可离线畅玩（仅加载激励广告等广告服务时需要网络连接）。您的闯关进度、星级与设置均保存在设备本地，卸载应用会清除本地存档。

Q5：如何管理广告隐私设置？

您可在游戏「设置」弹窗中点击「隐私政策」查看详情，或在支持地区点击「广告隐私设置」调整个性化广告授权；iOS 用户也可在系统「设置 > 隐私与安全性 > 跟踪」中随时管理权限。详见《隐私政策》。

English

About Panda Hanzi: Chinese Match

Panda Hanzi: Chinese Match (Bundle ID: com.pandahanzi.game) is a festive Chinese-character tile-matching puzzle game. Find two radicals or components on the board that combine into a real Chinese character (such as 冫 + 中 = 冲) and link them with a path of up to two turns!

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1: How do I play? What if I get stuck?

Tap two component tiles that form a valid Chinese character and can be connected with a line using no more than two 90-degree turns. If you get stuck, use the in-game Hint or Shuffle buttons. Every matched character displays its Pinyin pronunciation and meaning so you can learn as you play.

Q2: How do Hearts (energy) refill?

You can hold up to 5 Hearts. When below 5, 1 Heart regenerates automatically every 20 minutes. You can also tap the Heart icon at any time to watch a short rewarded ad and refill Hearts immediately.

Q3: How do I mute sound effects, music, or haptics?

Tap the Settings (设置) button in the top-right corner of the home screen to toggle Sound Effects, Music, and Vibration independently.

Q4: Does the game require an account? Where is my progress saved?

No account or sign-in is required. All level progress, stars, and settings are saved locally on your device. Please note that uninstalling the app will remove local save data.

Q5: How do I manage ad privacy or tracking settings?

On iOS, you can manage tracking permissions at any time under Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. On Android, go to Settings > Google > Ads. In supported regions, you can also tap Ad Privacy Settings (广告隐私设置) inside the in-game Settings sheet. Read our full Privacy Policy for details.
